# Graphics Atelier — programme map

**Proposed revision: 2026-11-20.** This is an organizational map of an existing research and production laboratory, not a declaration that a general graphical theory, professional method or aesthetic standard has been established. The original [charter](CHARTER.md), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [domain identifiers](DOMAINS.md), [research corpus](../research/QUEUE.md) and individual maturity records remain traceable.

## Governing question

**What relationships between artistic intention, representation, perception, computation, production and presentation make graphical quality possible, and how do they vary across media, technologies, institutions and historical circumstances?**

The practical ambition is to recover the capability to originate, construct, evaluate, revise, integrate and maintain sophisticated *original* graphics using accessible evidence and feasible tools. This does not reduce artistic judgment to scientific measurement or put historical fidelity above a contemporary target's actual needs. `Minimum quality` has two conditional meanings:

1. **Situated outcome adequacy:** the observable work meets the declared representational, expressive, interactive, accessibility and technical requirements of a specific use context.
2. **Productive capability:** the practitioner/workflow can reliably make, examine, modify and sustain outcomes at that level, with transparent evidence and costs.

Neither meaning assigns a global minimum pixel resolution, polygon count, palette, frame rate, style, nationality, fidelity to AM2, engine or aggregate aesthetic score. More complexity is not intrinsically more quality; intentional density or excess can nevertheless be justified by the intended experience.

## Relations of interest

- **Intention ↔ representation:** what the source artwork specifies, what a different raster/vector/polygon presentation can retain, exaggerate or omit.
- **Representation ↔ perception:** what information remains legible at the real camera, motion, background, screen and viewing circumstances; no human-outcome inference from positive pixel counts alone.
- **Art direction ↔ computation:** what parts of shape, movement, compositing and timing are formalizable, and what still requires situated author judgment and revision.
- **Production ↔ presentation:** how editable masters, roles, technical specifications, asset rights, transforms, interfaces and revisions survive through runtime.
- **Institutions ↔ technical history:** how craft procedures, tools, specialist labor, conventions and hard-won judgments are transmitted, modified, lost or rediscovered.
- **Constraints ↔ solutions:** how hardware budgets, software capabilities, artistic goals and organizational conditions produce locally excellent but not automatically universal methods.

The six Round 01 series retain independent sequencing. G02, G06, UI and the separate AM2 technical reconstruction retain their own evidence gates; cross-links do not transfer completed status.

## Programme governance

- Prefer the **outstanding question** to allegiance to a game, technology, studio or an overgrown prototype.
- Select a matching method: formal, empirical, practice-based, historical or production/organizational; several may apply together. [Methods](METHODS_AND_QUALITY.md) govern their evidential limits.
- Treat Japanese professional, doujin and independent literature as first-class evidence without inferring a unified national school. Seek pertinent counterexamples across media, regions and eras.
- Protect work already executed: IDs, paths, blob custody, source reports, negative results and original conclusions remain as written. Changes to interpretation are linked and dated, not silently backported.
- Separate **active portfolio** ([ACTIVE](../research/ACTIVE.md)) from **chronological handoff** ([HANDOFF](../HANDOFF.md)) and **source/case catalogs**.
- Commit to an executable or artistic test only when it resolves a bounded question. An experiment is not a requirement for every historical study; an algorithmic pass is not an artistic acceptance.
- Reserve integration for sufficiently qualified component methods, and demand a representative host, critique and revision before calling a method production-ready.
- Use historical fidelity as a research variable, not a universal acceptance criterion; AM2 is an exceptional case among many.
