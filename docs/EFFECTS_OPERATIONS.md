# Effects operations catalogue v0.1

2026-11-18, G06-A08. Provisional analytical vocabulary derived from the cited previous audits and original studies, not a historical Japanese taxonomy, tool API or complete generative grammar. An operation acts on a declared object (contour, part, field, phrase, clock or relation). A name alone is not an explanation. Composition and context determine what the operation does expressively.

Evidence references use the existing source registry; source-derived observations were qualified in G06-A01–05, but original films and exact frames remain unmeasured. Local experiment IDs do not certify every operation or generality. OP07 coalescence, OP13 reversal and physically grounded material transport remain especially weakly exercised. Catalogue includes analytical distinctions as well as authored operations.

## Composition contract

For each effect record:
- **Design**: intended reading or autonomous compositional purpose; foreground/background relation; references and alternatives.
- **Phrase**: ordered and/or parallel operations, local time, branch/trigger, parameters, input/output constituents and the invariant.
- **Representation**: drawing/exposure, procedural contour, rig, particle or shader; editable source and source→runtime mapping.
- **Context**: spatial coordinates, rendering priority, timing authority, necessary semantic cue (if any), ambiguity intentionally retained and cancellation rule.
- **Evidence**: actual output and comparison, revision cost, known defects, reproduction path and primary/secondary/own evidence level.

An operations graph is a design description, not necessarily an execution graph. Artist may draw a phrase directly rather than instantiate an emitter per label. Do not implement a universal effect language or build tooling merely because vocabulary exists.

## Example compositions

**Confluence:** converge → activate → expand; staggered attenuation; alternative cancel branch.
**Shearwake:** transport + deform + elongate → divide; shed independent slivers → attenuate/remove. Distinct authored and analytic contours.
**Interleave abstract:** two delayed recurrences; transport + converge spatially → divide → return to a single ribbon, with independent slivers; no gameplay trigger or semantic failure.
**Interleave intercept:** launch → directional transport; contact branches away from flight into short functional impact mark + optional splayed secondary fragments; miss continues to exit; cancel terminates without impact.

An effect family is not a fixed ordering of operations. Here the invariant for transposition is directional transport, conditional or composed separation, and independently moving remnants. Different contexts determine whether separation occurs; a miss deliberately does not reproduce the abstract phrase.
