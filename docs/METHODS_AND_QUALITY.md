# Methods, evidential jurisdictions and situated quality

**Programme clarification, 2026-11-20.** Applies alongside [PROTOCOL](PROTOCOL.md), not as a new global R maturity scale, artistic doctrine or demand for a scientific explanation of every craft result.

## A plurality of valid investigative methods

| Mode | It can establish, if actually supported | It does **not** establish by itself | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formal / computational** | Defined invariants, geometry, transformations, implementation behavior, numerical conditions, costs | Expressive quality or how historical practitioners actually worked | Derivation, source, controlled execution, test and performance data |
| **Empirical / perceptual** | Observer response or physical measurements *under declared stimuli and context* | Universal audience reaction, aesthetic desirability or production readiness | Viewing setup, controls, observations, participants/procedure when making human claims |
| **Practice-based / artistic** | How an author constructs, criticizes, alters and presents a particular intended visual quality | Audience-wide psychological efficacy, formal correctness outside checks, studio lineage | Editable originals, alternatives, full-context playback, artistic critique and revisions |
| **Historical / documentary** | Dated accounts, institutional techniques, transmission and change within source limits | A general causal genealogy from resemblance, or a modern method being mandatory | Primary artifacts, named reports, versioned secondary evidence, disagreements |
| **Production / organizational** | Handoff, roles, tool interoperability, asset continuity, labor, rework and economic conditions | Visual quality merely from a working toolchain | Asset contracts, pipeline logs, revision/import reruns, resource and role records |

These are **method families**, not insulated academic departments or grades of importance. A competent analysis may involve several, and a field practitioner may possess useful procedural knowledge without an explicit scientific theory. A scientific finding can constrain a design without determining artistic intent; art practice may expose phenomena for empirical study. Claims retain their own jurisdiction.

The existing [EK-A/B/C/D](PROTOCOL.md#evidence-kinds-not-ranked-solely-by-prestige) provenance classes, [R0–R5](CHARTER.md#reconstruction-states) operational maturity and optional [SEM ladder](EFFECTS_SEMIOTICS.md) are not replaced. Do not convert a source interview into an executed recipe, an editor-import success into a visual preference, or an intended emotional effect into tested reception.

## Two separate minimum-quality questions

**Outcome adequacy:** For this subject, scene, camera, display, audience, interaction and expressive goal, which properties are essential, which are deliberate concessions and what conditions would falsify adequacy? Criteria can include identity cues, timing, force, screen readability, dramatic hierarchy, accessibility and technical reliability; not all need numeric targets.

**Productive competence:** Can a practitioner or toolchain create the intended result and revise it without losing source rights, essential meaning, registration, presentation, or maintainability? Distinguish source editability, authoring skill, iterative capacity, recorded rework cost and technical execution.

Do not infer one from the other. A striking but uneditable prototype may pass the first and fail the second. A deterministic exporter may pass technical checks while delivering an inferior image. A simpler method may be preferable; additional complexity may also be artistically required. No universal lower bound on pixels, dimensions, detail, frame count, polygon budget or software is adopted.

## Decision record: five independent lanes where relevant

1. **Declared intent and use**: what experience, informational function or mechanical relation is required, in which scene/viewing conditions?
2. **Evidence and mechanism**: what is observed, documented, computed or inferred; whose source and which alternatives?
3. **Situated critique or observation**: does the actual output meet the intended character, timing, material, composition or legibility? Identify whether this is author judgment or a tested viewer outcome.
4. **Production and integration**: can the editable design be revised, exported, imported, triggered, canceled, repeated and maintained? What did the work cost?
5. **Disposition**: promote within qualified scope, revise, reject, defer or stop. Record negative findings and irreducible ambiguity.

No single aggregate score substitutes for an essential failed gate. Viewer testing is required only when asserting measured viewer effects, not every artistic craft conclusion. Representative context is necessary when it materially changes the evaluation; an isolated sprite or FX on black cannot validate crowded scene behavior.

## Selecting work rather than proliferating studies

Ask: **What exactly remains unknown, and what is the least demanding credible method to resolve it?** If an original specimen and unexecuted import test already exist, execute/inspect them before expanding the source survey absent a genuinely blocking gap. Conversely, source research is appropriate when a mechanism, provenance, right, technical limit or historical distinction is the open question.

For decisions with downstream effect, record alternatives, matched conditions, concrete failures and reversal criteria. Preserve original case evidence separately from contemporary implementations.
