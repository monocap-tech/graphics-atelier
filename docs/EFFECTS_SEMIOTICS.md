# G06-REV01 — Effects semantics and conventionalization
2026-11-18. User-directed revision after G06-RT01. Governs G06's next research priority; prior morphology, exposure, audiovisual and construction records retained as supporting studies. G07 remains deferred. No claim that animation, effects and semiotics have exclusive boundaries.

## Corrected research question

How do graphical, temporal, spatial, sonic and contextual relationships become sufficiently stable that an audience can interpret them, and how can an author deliberately exploit, extend, contrast or disrupt these relationships? Effects can make intangible relations, event consequences, material/emotional qualities and unreal phenomena perceptible. They can also be ambiguous, atmospheric or autonomous. No requirement that every effect encode a discrete message.

Separate the following:
- Form and behavior (what appears, moves, changes; animation and morphology).
- Sign function (what state, relation, source, quality, or possible action is inferred).
- Code/system (alternatives, invariants, exceptions, attachment, substitutions, combinations, hierarchy).
- Conventionalization (repeated associations, instruction, feedback, circulation across creators and media, audience learning; hypothesized processes requiring evidence).
- Production and transport (how the sign is authored and preserved through representation, export, runtime and revision).

The operation catalogue v0.1 is principally descriptive motion and compositional vocabulary. It is not a semantic dictionary, syntax or demonstrated generative language. Do not derive meaning from an operation's name or infer perceptual quality from technical assertions. Animation can be part of the sign itself, not merely a realization layer. Effects design also overlaps UI, illustration, sound, cinematography and other practices; difference here is analytical, not occupational exclusivity.

## Analysis levels and contrasts

1. Motif: shape, color, texture, support, motion/exposure, sound, scale, placement.
2. Attachment and reference: who/what bears the mark, who perceives it, source and recipient, field vs object vs screen, diegetic visibility or uncertain. Distinguish visible depiction from event rule and viewer reading.
3. Alternatives within a work: same event/different forms; different event/same form; same form/different attachment; relation between simultaneous signs; absent, misleading or ambiguous cue.
4. Code types (provisional): ordinal/quantitative; relational/conditional; categorical event/state; qualitative/affective; identity/register; atmospheric or nonpropositional. Types can coexist.
5. Scope: local learned rule, recurring franchise convention, broader genre/cross-media usage. Invariance within a game is not evidence of historical diffusion or universal audience comprehension.
6. Production obligation: which cue properties must survive changing actor, skin, stage, resolution, representation, timing or sound, and which may vary intentionally.

## Evidence ladder (orthogonal to R0–R5)

- S0 candidate motif or semantic claim, no qualified evidence.
- S1 attested rule, creator intent, instructional example or catalogued occurrence; distinguish these subtypes.
- S2 direct registered occurrence with timestamp/page, context and comparator; record uncertainty, no need to mirror rights-restricted imagery.
- S3 bounded comparative distribution (explicit sampling and coding; alternatives, negative cases, denominators where appropriate).
- S4 demonstrated reception, learning or transfer under appropriate procedure; historical transmission separately requires dated lineage evidence.

Use S2-static as an explicit qualifier for a registered still or crop without independently observed temporal sequence; retain S1 for meaning if supplied only by labels or documentation. S3 may describe a published external bounded corpus, not our own measurements; distinguish distribution from reception and historical lineage.

S levels are local provisional bookkeeping, not a psychometric scale and not automatically cumulative: a creator's intent and measured reception answer distinct questions. Do not sum levels or equate S4 with artistic merit.

## Research program

G06-S01: establish methodological corpus and qualified examples; see research/G06-SEMANTICS-01.md. Start from actual systems, not effect names. First source-led families Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (ordinal), Ikaruga (relational), Super Mario Odyssey (register); manga morphology (affective and relational), League comparator (identity and revision failure). Gunvolt, Guilty Gear and Mega Man Zero remain next expansion candidates. Japanese independent practitioners and player research remain first-class.

G06-S02: actual visual atlas. Register exact version/platform/scene or page, capture or source link, precise interval/figure, visual support and attachment, event/condition independently determined, contextual meaning and alternative, static/dynamic importance, other cues, negative case, source/rights/access. Start with four polarity pairing conditions plus switch/absorb/release observations in Ikaruga, and three tiers plus no-charge/release contrast in MK8DX; record both dark and bright contexts if feasible. Compare symbolic/realist impact or material treatment in Odyssey and at least one manga motif with a nonidentical arrangement. No false timestamps or image measurements. If access prevents direct cuts, document blocker and qualify textual/caption evidence only.

G06-S03: source-led cross-work comparison. Distinguish same semantic function with different forms and same form with divergent functions. Investigate conventions through dated records and negative cases, not chronological resemblance alone. Examine meaning that exceeds gameplay: emotion, magic/sacred/ominous registers, abstract atmosphere. Do not infer national uniformity or direct inheritance without evidence. Search 漫符, 形喩, 効果線, 記号性, 視覚言語, エフェクトの意味, 視認性, 属性, 演出, plus independent play-analysis and creator materials.

G06-S04: preregister original reconstruction using source-led contrasts. Hold animation fixed while changing assignment/attachment/context; hold semantic relation fixed while changing animation/representation; test static cue vs sequence vs with sound/feedback where appropriate. Compare simple baseline, intentional ambiguous or broken version and complete treatment. Distinguish formal decoder assertions from audience interpretation (no participant study performed yet). Select substantive target before making another prototype. Use RT01 requirements for engineering checks. Keep graphical construction and state truth outside audience labels.

G06-S05: real production handoff and transfer. Preserve meaning across a materially different effect, representation and host conditions, using actual DCC and runtime where available. Record costs, versions, asset handoff, revision, observed failures and controlled comparison; do not create a DSL prematurely.

Branch review: source-led visual atlas across material/interactive/nonliteral or affective families; bounded code/comparative evidence; original production with alternative/reference and meaningful semantic transfer; integration and repair gates of RT01. Do not make historical exhaustiveness or universal meanings terminal conditions. A code can be learned within one work; cross-work convergence and reception claims require additional evidence. G07 remains deferred pending deliberate review.

## S04 host/dependency qualification

See [S04a source precedents and static comparison](../research/G06-SEMANTICS-04A.md). Represented independent phenomenon, bearer-attached sign, intrinsic transformation of existing element, region/composite transformation and multiple-bearer relationships are analytical distinctions, orthogonal to semantic role and renderer architecture. A transition can operate on an existing identity without requiring a new independently represented object. Record host, coordinates, lifetime, dependency, continuity and semantic role separately; hybrid cases allowed. This is our synthesis, not a claimed single historical taxonomy. The static radiance experiment checks motif invariance, not audience interpretation or actual transitions.

## RT02 qualification — dependency and truth

Host (where drawn) is not necessarily referent (what is indicated). Record represented identity, spatial/causal/temporal dependencies, affected property, continuity, render mechanism and intended reading separately; these are overlapping analytical fields, not exclusive types. For state-bound cues, distinguish soundness from completeness, and transient event acknowledgment from persistent status. SRC180 explicitly reports a tutorial boss accepting counters beyond glint duration; no universal cue-window equality. See [RT02-R1](../research/G06-RT02-R1.md) and [LINK proposed comparison](../research/studies/G06-S04B-LINK.md). Regression correctness is not evidence of convergence or audience interpretation.
