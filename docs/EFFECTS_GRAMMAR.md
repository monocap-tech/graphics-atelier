## Research object

Effects as authored phenomena and transformations of the visual/audiovisual field, inside and outside games. They may convey state, imply force/material/scale, modify apparent space and time, establish atmosphere, or be an autonomous aesthetic object. An effect need not correspond to gameplay logic. An implementation primitive (particle, mesh, shader, drawing) does not determine the expressive grammar.

| G06.5 Representation and construction | frame drawing, cutouts, particles, animated geometry, simulation, procedural fields, shaders, texture/mesh methods and mixed approaches | implement a similar intent in at least two representations; quantify revision and resource tradeoffs |
| G06.6 Context and integration | game-state cue where required; UI, scene, camera, sound/music, environmental ambience and autonomous motion graphics where appropriate | consistent underlying event across different expressive treatments; same formal operation transposed across game and non-game contexts; actual compositing and interaction tests |

These categories overlap and are our analytical proposal, not a claimed historical Japanese classification. Investigate the source traditions separately: anime effects drawing (エフェクト作画), game effects (ゲームエフェクト), cinematography/compositing (撮影・特殊効果), graphic motion (モーショングラフィックス) and audiovisual composition. No claim of cultural transfer without evidence. Bullet x Ecology remains a neighboring research domain: movement/interaction of objects and hazards is not synonymous with their graphical expression, but selected behavior may itself be an expressive phenomenon.

## Target-driven study design

Prefer three different initial target families: (i) a material phenomenon such as smoke/flame/shock wave; (ii) a directed invented phenomenon such as a beam, burst or slash; (iii) a nonliteral graphic phrase such as a rhythmic ring, wipe or transformation that need not signify game state. Select exact lawful references and original specimens before committing. Names are illustrative and not an exhaustive effects taxonomy.

Record the source and intended context, visual/temporal primitives, relative order and duration, relations among layers, shape and density changes, spatial path, sync/offset with sound, alternative interpretation, implementation and display conditions. Separate directly observed phenomena, proposed causes, artist testimony and our synthesis.

One useful comparative lab: same geometry and color family, same total duration, two timing/spacing architectures (sharp impulse and slow dispersal vs gradual swell and abrupt cessation). Add a matched reference with phase order/relationship disrupted. Ask what remains physically/expressively coherent and what changes in interpretation. Then transpose an operation to a different medium or meaning; compare equivalent intent using frame drawing and parametric realization where feasible. A hypothesis, not evidence of a universal reading. Viewer tests require appropriate procedure and evidence; no claim from author intuition alone. Preserve original frames, time curves, versions and failures; no copied commercial assets.

## Source and acceptance rules

Commercial benchmarks, public animator techniques, independent blogs/open projects and observations of natural phenomena are all first-class leads; qualify each according to charter. Catalogue resources by actual operation/relationship and authoring method, not just names like "fire," "lightning" or product names. A publishable wiki record should expose repeatability, revision and attribution.

Provisional branch review gates: (1) bounded comparative atlas across the three target families with explicit sequence/shape observations and contrary cases; (2) operation and temporal-syntax catalogue grounded in qualified sources; (3) small original effect family with a reasoned alternate, two realization methods and at least one context transfer; (4) integration test for production-critical effects with G06-P01's state, timing, export and contrast gates, without imposing gameplay semantics on autonomous work. These are candidate readiness criteria, not a final comprehensive endpoint or certification. Evidence levels R0–R5 still apply per methodology. G07 can be revisited at a branch review or when needed as a dependency. Do not indefinitely postpone other tracks until all conceivable effects have been studied.
