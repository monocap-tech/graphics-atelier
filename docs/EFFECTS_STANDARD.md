# G06 — Effects production standard (STD01, v0.1)

Date: 2026-11-18. **Status: governing G06 domain standard.** This consolidates the [corpus-grounded review](../research/G06-BR01.md) and subsequent deep-research cross-audit. It does **not** claim new production results, psychological reception, historical studio reconstruction or a tool selection.

**Precedence:** [CHARTER](CHARTER.md) → [PROTOCOL](PROTOCOL.md) → **this domain standard** → supporting [GRAMMAR](EFFECTS_GRAMMAR.md), [SEMIOTICS](EFFECTS_SEMIOTICS.md) and [OPERATIONS](EFFECTS_OPERATIONS.md). Earlier REV01/REV02/G06-S07 priority statements remain historical decisions, not concurrent mandates. The [legacy crosswalk](../research/G06-STD01-CROSSWALK.md) governs interpretation of previous records; source IDs, experiments and observations are never silently rewritten.

## Object and boundary

**G06 reconstructs the independent craft of authoring, integrating, evaluating, revising and transferring visual or audiovisual phenomena and transformations that contribute to represented events/worlds, dramatic or semantic articulation and/or intended player/spectator experience.**

Effects may be naturalistic, invented, symbolic, atmospheric, abstract, interactive or autonomous; they need not be discrete objects, particles or game-state signals. This is a domain-specific application of the **target-to-workflow record** already mandated by PROTOCOL, not a replacement unit based on a semantic situation or an operation name. An isolated mathematical/technical fixture may answer a bounded research question, but does not establish a production method.

Four **interdependent facets** of each authored treatment, none a necessary causal first step:

| Facet | Record | Non-equivalence |
|---|---|---|
| Phenomenal / representational | What appears or changes: shape, material appearance, motion, depth, exposure, compositing, sound if relevant | Not necessarily faithful physical simulation |
| Situational / dramatic / semantic | What quality, circumstance, action possibility, identity or relation is articulated, for whom and when; may be absent, ambiguous or constituted by presentation | Not always a binary fact, learned code or gameplay variable |
| Experiential / psychological **intention** | Targeted attention, impact, responsiveness, anticipation, unease, exhilaration, tenderness, beauty, clarity, satisfaction, etc. | An author's goal, **not** evidence of actual viewer response |
| Production / implementation | Source illustration/textures/meshes, drawing or procedural authorship, tools, timing authority, export, performance, revision and runtime handoff | Passing code tests alone is not artistic success |

This is not an exhaustive psychology, a national effects taxonomy, a one-way pipeline or an exclusive occupational definition. All four can interact with illustration, animation, lighting, cinematography, UI, sound and game behavior. A scene may succeed without any added effect.

## Terminology and claim discipline

Record source-native terminology and working translation, rather than standardizing away differences: エフェクト (*efekuto*, effect), 効果 (*kōka*, effect/result), 影響 (*eikyō*, influence), エフェクト作画 (*efekuto sakuga*, effects drawing), ゲームエフェクト, 撮影/特殊効果, screen effects, VFX, hit effects. Nintendo explicitly describes effects as enhancing game feel, intuitive comprehension and visual richness [SRC087](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/introduction/design/work04.html). This documents current professional usage, **not why the English historical label was coined**. The 2011 CEDEC discussion of drawn/game effects and expressive smoke is qualified professional testimony [SRC099](https://www.4gamer.net/games/131/G013104/20110906084/). Sega's 2019/2025 training descriptions ground staged construction, constraints and scene-relative critique [SRC102](https://cgworld.jp/interview/201911-segagames.html), [SRC200](https://cgworld.jp/article/202505-cgkiso-2.html). Source descriptions cannot substitute for observed original cuts, the artist's editable project or tested audience response.

Use the narrowest defensible verb: **documented by creator**, **directly observed in media**, **implemented in our fixture**, **judged in author critique**, **reported by viewers under stated procedure**, **integrated and rerun**. Do not promote an intended meaning or psychological effect into measured reception. Controlled studies such as [2026 screen-vibration combinations](https://doi.org/10.57518/digrajprocsummer.2026.0_11) or [Kao 2020 juiciness](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.entcom.2020.100359) are context-bound external findings, not presets or evidence that G06 achieved their outcomes.

## Canonical record: target → workflow → decision

This extends, but does not duplicate, PROTOCOL's minimum record. A concise heading block plus linked artifacts is sufficient; use `unknown`, `not tested` or `not applicable` explicitly. No obligation to assign numerical ratings to aesthetic judgments.

| Field | Minimum entry |
|---|---|
| **Identity and status** | Stable ID, name, domain, explicit `proposed/active/executed-bounded/held/blocked/deferred/rejected/closed`, R-level **with scope**, applicable TC conditions |
| **Reference and provenance** | Exact work/version/platform/cut/timestamp or page, source IDs and type, language, lawful access/rights, **text inspected vs image vs timed video vs code vs testimony**, missing materials |
| **Target property and constraint** | Observable properties, experiential artistic brief, intended use, scene demands, simpler alternative and explicit non-goals |
| **Phenomenal treatment** | Visual/sonic constituents, shapes/materials, motion/exposure/phase, space, composition, layering, drawing/particle/mesh/shader approaches |
| **Situational relation (if relevant)** | Participants, host/referent, represented consequence, selective disclosure/ambiguity, competing readings; `autonomous/unspecified` valid |
| **Authoring custody** | Exact tools/versions, source art and licenses, editable projects, assets, authored vs generated steps, revision lineage, export settings |
| **Runtime contract** | Coordinate/anchor space, timing/trigger/cancel policy, blending/depth/color, audio coupling if used, import/export and reset/replay |
| **Representative viewing** | Camera, scale, background, contrast, clutter, simultaneously active effects, movement, platform/display and full-sequence playback |
| **Comparators** | Plain/restrained control and one plausible adverse scene condition; note matched and unmatched variables, negative results |
| **Costs** | Actual or estimated labor and revision count; platform-dependent memory/overdraw/draw calls/performance where relevant; mark absent measures |
| **Four evidence lanes** | (1) technical correctness, (2) situated artistic critique, (3) audience/viewer reception if performed, (4) production readiness/integration. Each with evidence and limitation |
| **Transfer and decision** | Distinct asset/context for essential method or justified exception; promote/revise/reject/defer/stop, uncertainty and next bounded question |

**Minimum evaluative distinction:** an accurate mask is not an expressive achievement; an author's judgment of force is not a viewer study; a tested viewer impression is not proof of editable/maintainable production. Positive results in one lane do not compensate for unpassed essential gates.

### Prefixes and historical compatibility

Original meanings persist. New/updated records use:

- **EK-A/B/C/D** = PROTOCOL evidence kinds A documented original method, B transferable professional method, C inference/independent reconstruction, D our independently tested method. Multiple kinds may apply.
- **R0–R5** = charter maturity, **record-and-scope specific**, never a blanket branch rank. An R3 fixture does not demonstrate an R4 general method.
- **TC-A/B/C** = charter terminal epistemic/operational/production conditions, **not** EK-A/B/C.
- **SEM-S0…SEM-S4** = the optional SEMIOTICS ladder for sign/interpretation claims, not a mandatory requirement for nonsemantic or purely artistic work. Its S levels are not an ordinal rating of artistic quality and not necessarily cumulative.
- **Status** = explicit workflow word, independent of R-level. An executed fixture can remain blocked for transfer.

Keep legacy unprefixed labels readable through this crosswalk; preserve original wording and test results. R3 test certification never implies SEM-S4 reception, TC-B or TC-C.

## Production and quality gates

| Gate | Requirement before claiming it passed | If unavailable or failed |
|---|---|---|
| **FX-T target** | Identifiable reference-backed target *or* expressly original professionally informed brief; observable property, desired experienced quality and control. Distinguish documented source brief from frame-accurate target reconstruction | Label as independent interpretation; do not claim matched-source fidelity |
| **FX-A authorship** | Substantive original, editable source in an actual artist-usable workflow; authored intermediate drawings/textures/geometry/graphs, reproducible export | A code-only toy remains a bounded method/fixture |
| **FX-S scene** | Full sequence shown against representative camera, animation, geometry, background, action density and screen scale | Black-background or static-only result is not a scene validation |
| **FX-C comparison** | Restraint/on-off alternative, one adverse condition; deliberate visual critique of weight, shape, pacing, attention, legibility, dramatic quality, coherence; limits/competing explanations | Record negative critique, revise or stop; passing a regression suite is not enough |
| **FX-R revision** | At least one documented artistic edit propagated through source → export → representative playback/import with cost and problem identified | Unproven iteration and handoff |
| **FX-I integration** | Real editor/runtime import, trigger/timing/cancel/reset if applicable, intended color/depth/compositing and performance checks, reproducible rerun | No production integration claim |
| **FX-X transfer** | If essential/recurring: independently adapt the method to a **materially different** specimen or context, or document why exception justified | Same geometry with cosmetic recoloring is not independent transfer |
| **FX-V reception** | **Only when claiming actual psychological response/interpretation/preference:** viewers, procedure, question, controls and scope explicitly documented | Intention and critique remain labeled as such; not a default hurdle for craft studies |

Technical invariants (alpha, correct host, branch/cancel, blend, timing, export identity) merit independent checks or mutation tests **when they threaten production correctness**. Do not turn a simple artistic study into an unlimited oracle campaign. Prefer a bounded build → full playback → critique → revision → import → decision loop over further isolated semantics prototypes. After a representative attempt, explicitly stop, revise, transfer or escalate; no obligation to exhaust all phenomena.
