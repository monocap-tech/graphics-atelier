# Documentation and future wiki

Canonical material lives in version-controlled Markdown alongside original evidence and small reproducible examples, with stable IDs for sources, targets, studies and decisions. This leaves a later wiki/site generator open (MkDocs, Docusaurus or another suitable tool). Do not treat a separate wiki as a second editable source of truth.

A future public documentation site can expose a reference atlas (links and original annotations), research corpus, methodology catalogue, experimental archive, tooling library and graphics specification. Navigation should be by capability, target, maturity, and cross-reference, not chronology alone.

Public records use original analysis, links, short justified excerpts with attribution, and our own or explicitly licensed assets. Preserve source licenses and attribution per artifact. Do not mirror paid texts, ripped game assets, proprietary internal documents, third-party fonts or recordings without permission. Source availability does not imply redistribution rights. No default project license is inferred from creation of the repository.

Private notebooks and prior private curricula remain outside this public repository unless explicitly cleared for publication. Review rights and accuracy before publishing each record; label hypotheses, candidate procedures and failed experiments visibly. No site is deployed by this foundation.
