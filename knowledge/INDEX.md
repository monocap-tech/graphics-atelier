## Evidence handling

- **R0–R5** = maturity of a *specific claim/workflow*, not a rank assigned wholesale to a file. **EK-A/B/C/D** = original method / transferable professional method / independent inference-reconstruction / our experiment. Check [PROTOCOL](../docs/PROTOCOL.md) for exact definitions.
- Separate: (a) mechanically verified properties; (b) artistic interpretation and author critique; (c) observed viewer outcome, if any; (d) actual runtime/production readiness. Passing one lane does not pass the others.
- Distinguish rights to inspect a reference from rights to copy artwork or distribute a vendor's editor or method.
- Report an unexecuted stage as **pending/blocked**, not “passed by design.”
