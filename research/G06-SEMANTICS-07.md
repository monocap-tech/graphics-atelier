## Historical and situated contrast

- 2017-era DMM theater: audience participates in a staged relation even though the reviewed interaction is apparent rather than computational. Source is critic testimony; do not assume all systems of this production identical.
- 2018 cinema: producers explicitly treat care for another as the referent of light (including audience-character and character-character relationships); theatrical projection and audience light/voice together constitute presentation.
- 2020 cinema: producer asks for inward support without physical light-waving. Negative case for the necessity of one physical sign carrier; not an equivalence study or assertion that on-screen effects were removed.
- 2023 VR: spectators placed inside town as residents, support articulated as illumination and blooming ground. Attested recurrence/adaptation within franchise by production report; not proof of exact historical transfer mechanism for every graphic operation.

Distinguish (i) real action of spectator, (ii) actual technical dependency on action, (iii) represented causal relationship in fiction, (iv) experienced participation, (v) interpersonal meaning and (vi) graphics implementation. These need not coincide. A prerecorded scene can articulate solidarity without simulating it, and a physically real input can provide poor dramatic articulation. No false claim of real audience input changing VR show outcome. Do not collapse care into transferable energy/resource, or narrative significance into perceived signal clarity.
