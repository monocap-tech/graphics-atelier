# G06-F02 — The manga page as constructed reading and expressive composition

Date: 2026-11-18. **Status: bounded source-led observation; R1 practice descriptions and R1 direct visual inspection of two small publisher teaching samples.** This follows [F01](G06-FUNDAMENTALS-01.md). It does not claim R3 reproduction, measured readers, full-chapter analysis, or direct manga→anime→games genealogy. No prototype, external asset acquisition, engine work, format standard, production target or psychological test performed. Source images remain at publishers' URLs; no copyrighted panels mirrored into the repository.

## The question

Before isolating effects (効果線, トーン, 漫符, etc.), how is a manga page designed so that panel arrangement, reading order, balloon placement, figures, scale, backgrounds, marks and finishing operate together? A manga page is not reducible to a sequence of independent illustrations. This is a source-grounded working proposition, not an empirically measured eye-tracking universal. Reading convention and physical book orientation are explicit assumptions of the sources.

## Two directly inspected page-based records

### F02-V01 — One manga teaching page through four stages (Shogakukan)

**Source/custody:** ©十神 真 for publisher's teaching sample; publisher URLs F02-SRC1. The images are roughly postage-stamp reproductions, four stages of a single example composition, not a full scene sequence. They retain a large upper bust portrait, a stack of smaller lower panels, and an enlarged standing figure on the right with nearby balloons. Source image quality does not justify precise measurements of ink/tonal area or transcription of all dialogue.

- **ネーム:** rough allocations, figures, balloons, main focus and radiating line direction already visible. The top portrait occupies much of horizontal page extent; subordinate exchanges and long/right-hand figure coexist below.
- **下描き:** anatomy, perspective, pose and concentration-line trajectory are refined; reading infrastructure remains visibly in place, not introduced only after drawing.
- **ペン入れ:** panel frames, focus rays and foreground figures become crisp; lettered marks/background architecture appear as finished ink. Several balloons are visibly empty in the instructional view.
- **仕上げ:** dark hair/fabric masses and gray-tone regions supply visual hierarchy and material differentiation; broad page configuration remains recognizable.
- **Inference (bounded):** a finishing mark works inside an earlier established hierarchy. Artist could nonetheless alter the plan at late stage (F02-SRC7); this one image does not prove universal fixity of name or exact drawing order.
- **Unmeasured:** actual reader eye paths, temporal impression, print moiré, full dialogue, other pages and the artist's labor.

### F02-V02 — Same coastal situation, changed spatial priority (Shogakukan)

**Source/custody:** illustrative alternative page compositions in F02-SRC2, visually inspected [A](https://shincomi.shogakukan.co.jp/eximages/training/060904_6a.jpg) and [B](https://shincomi.shogakukan.co.jp/eximages/training/060904_6b.jpg). This is a controlled **pedagogical illustration**, not a controlled participant experiment or documented published comic revision.

- **A:** beach/coast is a small rightmost tall panel alongside a pair of closer character depictions in the upper row. Environment and person are both readable; the setting is not the largest single compositional unit.
- **B:** beach scene becomes a broad top panel with a single small standing figure; woman close-up is lower. The composition allocates more screen/page area to coastline and distant lighthouse.
- **Publisher's interpretation:** use B when the beach itself and the figure's relation to it must register strongly, A for routine setting/location information.
- **Our inference:** the primary manipulated lever is *page composition/panel area and framing*, not addition of a named effect. Radial emphasis around the woman in both illustrations does not independently explain the changed hierarchy. The line treatment itself cannot be treated as unaltered at precise raster level; compare broad visual function, not pixel equivalence.
- **Unmeasured:** which reader notices what, eye movement, a production worker's edit path or a causal effect size of panel scale.

## Foundational craft claims the examples support

1. **ネーム is operational, not merely a low-resolution drawing.** It is an editable plan for sequence, panel sizes, dialogue and composition. The images and creator interviews demonstrate that professional production may allocate expressive decisions before detailed illustration.
2. **Paneling is an active production operation.** コマ割り controls what is large, subordinate, adjacent, simultaneous or withheld across a printed spread. 構図 controls treatment within the frame; both must be considered together. Changing either can reorganize dramatic weight with no added 'VFX'.
3. **Reader guidance is distributed.** A page can enlist face, gaze, balloon, text, lettering, line direction, panel frame/spacing, white space and page-turn. Right-to-left, top-to-bottom and narrow horizontal/wider vertical gutters are the publisher's recommended default for the assumed right-opening printed edition. Shogakukan suggests ~4–6 mm narrow vs ~8–10 mm wide gutters and around five panels/page; treat these as **local pedagogy**, not industry constants, scaling law, metric-derived aesthetic universals or validated psychophysics.
4. **The medium's enclosure is part of authorship.** Book spine/gutter (ノド), trim/bleed (タチキリ), page turn, monochrome reproduction, and for smartphone serialization the viewport/scroll encounter affect artwork and timing. Source F02-SRC7 explicitly names 'first-glance impression' as a personal editorial priority, not reception data.
5. **The procedure is not single-file.** Shogakukan's idealized name→pencil→ink→finish is compatible with Sukeno's page/balloon-first procedure, Watsuki's planning tones at name, and Michelle's late revisions. The order of operations in tutorial is a descriptive example, not a universally fixed craft chain.
6. **Effects are not required to be independent assets.** A line/radial field can function as part of framing; hand-lettered sounds may be both text and compositional shapes; black/tone masses can operate as form or atmosphere. This corroborates F01's separability caveat but does not prove any direct genealogical path into game VFX.

## What this does *not* establish

Not a random or representative sample of manga; the direct source stills are small and from explicitly educational examples. We did not inspect a complete chapter or measure actual reader gaze, fixations, emotion or tempo. We did not examine historical first appearances or studio-to-studio personnel transfer. Do not claim that panels are equivalent to animation frames or that screen-relative visual effects in games inherit exactly the same spatial reading rules. No property of actual commercial manga is 'validated' by the official tutorial alone.
