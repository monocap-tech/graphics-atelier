## Design implications
Record host, referent, dependency, represented identity, affected property, presentation policy, implementation and meaning separately. A cue can carry truth without exhaustively enumerating a state. Optional residue must not be confused with a live action affordance. A persistent interface status and transient acknowledgment are not aliases. Semantic effects need not all have mechanical truth conditions.
