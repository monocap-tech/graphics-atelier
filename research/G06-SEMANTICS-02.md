- Black backgrounds .047 / .007

- Flower petals .001 / .021
- Fuzzy circles .002 / .026
- Chibi .035 / .138

All above rows marked p<.05 by authors. Exploratory selection from reported comparisons, no multiple-testing correction verified, not independently controlled for narrative event opportunities or exposure. The paper reports 2711 schemas, 3191 occupied panels of 5007, and mean 1.49 schemas per occupied panel. These aggregate numbers cannot all be correct under a single counting convention (2711 < 3191 and division is not 1.49); no correction guessed. Use reported schema-type presence and Table 1 values as explicitly qualified source claims. No extrapolation of occurrence rate, total counts, robust significance or national prevalence. ## Semantic implications (hypotheses)

**Shared sign repertoire vs style distribution:** a style can select and combine common signs with distinctive relative emphasis. Alternative explanations include different depicted event opportunities and genres; corpus frequencies do not prove expressive preferences after conditioning on scenes. Need compare matched emotional/dramatic situations.

**Attachment and support:** VA01 attaches pain to a real photographed body part, VA02–04 change the surrounding field, VA05 to a vehicle's moving wheels, VA06–07 associate entity and projectile families through appearance. Position is part of the sign construction. It need not be represented as a literal object or material.

**A code across multiple constituents:** Ikaruga pairs dark centers with warm outlines and pale luminous shots with blue-white affiliation; literal palette names black/white compress actual appearance. Ship enclosure helps expose relationship. Do not equate glow color with absolute danger. Same-state alternative shape/luminance, high-density and grayscale comparison pending.

**Versioned convention:** Wii U MK8 manual and Switch MK8DX announcement attest preservation plus extension of an ordinal code. RC series differences are a hypothesis for next source audit; do not confuse color labels across games. Within-work designed consistency differs from cross-creator convention, audience learning and historical diffusion.

**Semantic layer beyond animation:** VA01 deploys conventional forms on static photography. Motion may enrich or constitute another sign, but it is not necessary in this case. Static evidence cannot demonstrate temporal phase relationships. Shared motif across titles need not mean historical transmission.
