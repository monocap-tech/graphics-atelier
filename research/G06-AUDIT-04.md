## Synthesized relational distinctions (our hypothesis/framework)

1. Event coincidence (onset/offset and lag), which is not sufficient for a consistent audiovisual mapping.
2. Source/motif identity, including stable reassignment of a source to a new visual motif.
3. Phrase structure: recurrence, variation, inversion, segmentation, lead/lag, counterpoint; not just strength proportional to an audio envelope. A delayed or ambiguous correspondence can be intentional.
4. Semantics/pragmatics: recognition and associations with source material, fictional identity, dramatic interpretation and operational reading.
5. Authority and realization: authored fixed score, signal-driven schedule, interactive event stream; combination with independently evolving scene/game state. A screen-relative mark need not represent a material object.

A musical analysis-to-game obstacle map is related but distinct from authored audiovisual effects. Register Vib-Ribbon as adjacent lead for later signal/geometry study; do not infer original algorithm or impose music-driven gameplay on reverse STG. UI Atelier's separate private records stay private. G08 can use the same conceptual distinctions with operational state mapping; autonomous effects need not indicate state.
