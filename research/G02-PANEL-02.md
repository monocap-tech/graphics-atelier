## Reading grammar is contingent

Natsume (2013), Nausicaä essay, names v1 p27, v3 p79, v4 p89 and other edition examples where Miyazaki alternates vertical groups and horizontal rows. Reports a classroom show-of-hands for v1 p27 (more than half taking a different route); not controlled eye tracking. ## Published negative result — spacing alone

Sumi Nanae (墨菜々絵) and Yamamoto Keiko (山本景子), IPSJ Interaction 2026 paper 2P72, pp.819–823, methods/results text and rendered PDF pp.820 and 822 inspected. Twelve small-B6 volumes (six per author-defined shonen/shojo category), selected pages with measurable gaps. Reported mean gap normal to vertical boundary 1.33 vs 0.92 mm (p=.145); normal to horizontal boundary 3.67 vs 2.67 mm (p=.044); zero-gap line-only delimitation observed in all six shojo volumes but none of six shonen. These are published-volume rather than B4 manuscript dimensions. Small selected sample, not universal genre distinction.

Study 2 is author-only pilot. Subsequent participant experiment 1, n=23, three original vertical triptychs, 1–7% page-width gap, 21 stimuli; no significant pairwise difference in reported tension. Experiment 2, n=36, original three-panel sequences, two scenarios × six expressions × 0 or ~3% gap; no significant difference for matched emotion rating. Fig.6 demonstrates facial expression change under common layout, Fig.7 near-similar paired means. No claim spacing has zero effect generally; narrow manipulation, restricted content, age skew and few experienced artists limit inference. No raw dataset or replication.

2020 IEICE Watanabe/Negishi/Izuhara abstract suggests text/balloons may outrank other gaze cues, and concentration lines may have psychological effects beyond gaze changes. 2019 Murakami/Sumi project page and independent conference attendee report describe eye tracking of irregular layouts; original hypothesis not straightforwardly supported. Full papers not read; no effect size or universal priority asserted.

## Candidate notation / next corpus increment

A local aperture P_i has region, border policy, role, image support, cue anchors, transform and visibility; add an intended reading precedence relation i≺j (allow partial order), depicted event relation (may be simultaneous), and directional/semantic continuity edges (shared object, motion/gaze vector, contrast, analogy). Reading precedence, depicted time, page visibility and observed scanpath must remain distinct. Geometric form alone cannot establish semantic meaning.

Next bounded increment: independently inspect a lawful shōjo layering specimen and a four-panel comedy/strip specimen (different temporal organization); annotate visible layer and reading alternatives, then compare with PN02 cases. Continue separate aperture source-frame/runtime question; no new prototype or G06 cursor replacement. Published experimental limitations constrain later design evaluations; retain matched-content comparisons and do not claim effects from geometry alone.
