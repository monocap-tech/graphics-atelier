## Discovery targets

Gunvolt (raster silhouette and motion); KOF XII/XIII (3D-assisted sprites); Radiant Silvergun and Ikaruga (hybrid geometry, camera and composition); RayStorm (polygonal stage); ESP Ra.De. (projectile readability against urban imagery); Key-era ADV background illustration (lighting and composition). These are candidate benchmarks, not a style prescription or historical attribution. Register individual frames/segments and lawful reference locations before experimentation.
