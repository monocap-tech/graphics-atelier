# G02-DIMENSIONAL-01 — American design-led metrication and representational coherence

2026-11-19 | Parallel intake for GA-R01-S06 / G02; bounded R1 documentary findings, R0 hypotheses and acquisition leads. No new series launch or prototype.

## Question

Did American practitioners and institutions themselves identify dimensional incompatibility across design, architectural documentation, print reproduction and presentation, independently of our existing Vignelli inspiration? Investigate from design practice backwards through its enabling dimensional and production systems, not from national stereotypes or a presumed European/Japanese superiority. US customary inch-pound units are not identical to British Imperial units.

## Three distinct dimensions of coherence (proposed analytical distinction, R0)

- Metrological: units and conversion policy, including exact inch/mm conversion versus convenient rounded dimensions.
- Dimensional: shape families, modules, subdivision, tolerances, packing and interoperability; metric notation alone does not impose these. Ideal A-series aspect ratio is sqrt(2) (actual millimetre sheet dimensions rounded); repeated halving with rotation preserves the ideal ratio. Letter halving does not preserve its ratio. A ratio family is independent of the measurement unit.
- Representational: whether an authoritative visual specification survives transformations between editable master, print/presentation aperture, raster assets and final display, including intentional reinterpretation, hierarchy, anchors and protected regions.

Soft conversion changes expression of a dimension; hard conversion changes physical design dimensions. Distinguish both from switching aspect-ratio families and from a composition redesign. The useful general analogy is interface/production contract migration: installed tooling, shared formats, interdependent stages, waste/rework and design intent must all be accounted for. This is an analogy, not demonstrated historical descent.
