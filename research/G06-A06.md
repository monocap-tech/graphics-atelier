## Authorial brief

Compose an impression of several independent components becoming one directed act, then separating again. The intended expressive character is measured, restrained, precise and energetic without a screen-filling blast. Original vocabulary: three cyan arcs and advancing tips, concentric contraction, luminous center, amber expanding ring fragments, staggered residual traces. The invariant is gathering into a focal event followed by dispersal; not literal machinery. The three contexts are deliberately related: autonomous abstract composition, a focused mechanical discharge, and a targeting cue. In the latter two, an additional beam or reticle-center mark conveys an active condition, while cancellation suppresses that mark. The abstract use has no gameplay obligation. Color choices, timings, graphic treatment and screen sizes were chosen locally by the assistant as experimental decisions, not user-approved art direction.

The transfer is not yet proof of independent authorship across widely separated registers. All three share geometry and a deterministic timeline; the second realization is a different sampling regime, not independently hand-drawn animation. No actual fighter, targeting sensor, audio, scene simulation, Unity or editor integration. Critique: some convergence/activation transitions are abrupt, the beam currently sustains a simple bar, the residue reads faintly on the test background, and these contexts risk being cosmetic adaptations of one effect. Further work must create a genuinely contrasting morphological family, change composition beyond icon substitution, and compare alternative representations using actual editable drawings or independently built particle/geometry structures.
