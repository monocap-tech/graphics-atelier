# GA-R01-S02 — Deformation, identity and representational appeal

**Status:** chartered / R0 (2026-11-18); no new comparison or perception result.

**Core question:** How can デフォルメ (*deformé*: selective redesign, simplification or exaggeration) communicate a character's identity or appeal more effectively in a new medium, rather than merely approximating a detailed original?

## Research territory

- Distinguish source fidelity, character recognizability, character-specific appeal, intended expression, silhouette and stylistic coherence. None is a surrogate for the others.
- Compare shape ratios, large color regions, asymmetric cues, controlled omissions, substitution marks, facial expression and pose-specific deviations.
- Follow the same character through high-detail illustration, compact dot art, chibi treatment, 3D model, animation and modern remakes where source/production accounts are available.
- Explore not only 'what survives?' but 'what can the new representation do that the source could not?' and the constraints on legitimate redesign.

## Opening questions

1. Which cues are stable across media, and which are deliberately reauthored?
2. At what point does simplification become a redesign, and who approves the change?
3. Are the artist's declared intentions and viewers' actual recognitions aligned?
4. Can a low-resolution version amplify posture, personality or legibility at the cost of literal anatomy?

## Evidence and methods

Use the same-character 128/32/24-pixel comparisons by [有織はべり](https://note.com/ari_ori/n/nd77e2c3fe470), the Shibuya and SNK production leads in [G02-FEATURE-01](../../G02-FEATURE-01.md), and independently verify the previously discussed *Theatrhythm* designer commentary before quoting it. Make side-by-side matched viewing conditions explicit; distinguish authored version changes from automatic resampling and different camera poses. Viewer recognition tests are optional and required only for audience-efficacy claims.

## Outputs and limits

An annotated atlas of transformation decisions and an original two-size character redesign contrast are candidates. No generalized 'appeal function', national style thesis or universal feature ranking follows from isolated examples. Document disagreements and intentionally unsuccessful simplifications.

**First bounded inquiry:** one character with at least three authorized depictions and available artist remarks; identify five explicit decisions, including one changed relationship that is not merely a lost detail.
