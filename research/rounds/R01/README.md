## Governance: room to breathe

- **Series are independent:** a finding or blocker in one does not halt the others. Numbers are identifiers, **not** rank or scheduled order. Research may proceed sequentially for attention management without imposing conceptual dependencies.
- **No premature synthesis:** do not claim a common Japanese design philosophy, a universal feature hierarchy, or a unified automatic graphics pipeline just because branches share terms.
- **Studies are subordinate to questions, not vice versa:** each series may acquire several studies and change its internal outline after primary-source contact. Start with an open question; choose its methods from what the evidence requires. Do not prescribe identical experimental formats or rigid paper quotas.
- **One record per actual investigation:** author new files `S0x-01-<slug>.md`, `S0x-02-<slug>.md` alongside the charter; each cites sources and [investigation protocol](../../../docs/PROTOCOL.md) EK categories / R maturity. Retain negative results, contrary evidence, corrections and unresolved source access.
- **Cross-series findings travel with provenance:** when a result informs a second series, link to the original record rather than silently copying or upgrading its evidence level.
- **Prior work is evidence, not automatic closure:** [G02-FEATURE-01](../../G02-FEATURE-01.md), the Japanese book inspections and G02 atlas remain bounded intake. The same material can be reexamined more closely in an individual series.
- **Separate investigations remain separate:** Suzuki/AM2 technical reconstruction belongs to its other thread; G06 effects' current work and G07 deferral are not superseded by Round 01. The GameObjects/Unity course is not the research charter.
- **Rights and source discipline:** do not add scans, commercial asset crops or protected art to the public repository without permission. Use public links, independently drawn specimens and measurements with precise provenance.
- **No automatic schedule or completion claim:** do not assign deadlines, results, or production status until an individual series has actually begun and its evidence has been inspected.

## How a series advances

A series can remain in discovery across multiple papers/interviews, pause for artifact acquisition, run an original bounded test, compare conflicting production accounts, or stop at a qualified descriptive result. R0–R5 apply to **specific studies and claims**, not as a stamp earned by naming a series. Each study should end with a precise question, evidence state, worthwhile next inquiry and a decision to continue/pause/stop. Existing [G02-P01](../../studies/G02-P01.md) provides one candidate original-art integration test but is **not** a gate that every series must clear.
