# GA-R01-S01 — Illustration specification and design custody

**Status:** active / opened 2026-11-18. The first source-led study [GA-R01-S01-01](S01-01-source-packets.md) is qualified at R1 for bounded commercial and independent brief observations; no complete sprite-delivery packet or production specimen is validated.

**Core question:** What must a character/illustration specification communicate so that different practitioners can produce consistent, lawful and editable representations without treating the finished illustration as an exhaustive truth?

## Research territory

- 設定画, キャラクターデザイン, 三面図, 身長対比表, 表情集, 色指定, 指示書, 作画監修; production vocabulary is a search map, not a claim that every studio uses identical documents.
- Source acquisition, original commissioning, reference and provenance custody, layered masters, change approval, contractual rights, representational deviations, revision propagation.
- Information absent from one image: hidden construction, asymmetry, narrative-significant ornament, mood/character expression, shared palette constraints, animation requirements.
- Two-way handoff: illustration↔sprite↔model↔portrait/cut-in↔runtime; distinguish specification, depiction, and deliverable.

## Opening questions

1. Which design decisions are made before a character drawing, which are encoded in a model sheet, and which emerge in downstream adaptations?
2. What evidence demonstrates that a detail is identity-bearing or story-bearing rather than just decorative?
3. How do rights, editable layers, color/pose specifications and revision authority survive a multi-artist handoff?
4. When is a sprite-led illustration or a revisable design preferable to a one-way 'convert this illustration' pipeline?

## Evidence and methods

Begin with complete examples of lawful public production briefs, artist instruction and handoff material; record creator, period, original language, actual artifacts, and whether passages are contemporary or retrospective. Professional interviews (including the previously discussed Akatsuki illustration-production lead) require exact passage qualification. Compare multiple media and independent art teams rather than imposing a studio-specific template. Where possible test a small *original* specification with an artist-role handoff: change an accessory, propagate to two representations and record ambiguities.

## Outputs and limits

A comparative specification atlas, vocabulary, variant-approval rubric and a minimal rights-aware editable brief are **candidate outputs**, not a required immediate deliverable. Any 'best practice' requires more than one company's anecdote. Crosslink [G02-P01](../../studies/G02-P01.md) and [G02-FEATURE-01](../../G02-FEATURE-01.md); do not confuse this with G06's effects scene authoring.
