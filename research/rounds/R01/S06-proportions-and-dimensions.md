# GA-R01-S06 — Proportional and dimensional conventions

**Status:** chartered / R0 (2026-11-18), with pre-existing bounded records in G02-AUDIT-01 and G02-ATLAS-01.

**Core question:** How do character anatomy, native sprite dimensions, source sheet/workspace geometry, viewport occupation and display-scale choices constrain one another across concrete production targets?

## Research territory

- Head/body ratios (頭身), world-to-raster mappings, frame/atlas envelopes, camera, on-screen height, motion excursion and UI/scene co-occupancy.
- Reported artist grids, native output classes, paper/layout conventions, metric units, resolution changes and handheld/arcade/HD targets.
- The difference between source drawing size, registration frame, opaque bounds, logical viewport, actual game-world viewport, display enlargement and cropped publicity screenshot.
- Test golden/silver ratios, JIS A/B paper and metric influence **as separate falsifiable historical hypotheses**, not presumed explanations.

## Opening questions

1. Which nominal asset sizes correspond to measured gameplay occupancy for a stated camera and platform?
2. What is gained or lost by holding screen occupancy constant while changing native density, versus holding density constant and changing camera?
3. Do designers specify ratios explicitly or arrive at them through anatomy, readability, background scale and animation labor?
4. What historical documents actually connect printing/layout standards to game sprite practice, if any?

## Evidence and methods

Continue [G02-AUDIT-01](../../G02-AUDIT-01.md) and [G02-ATLAS-01](../../G02-ATLAS-01.md), with [G02-P01](../../studies/G02-P01.md) as the experimental contract. Register game/version/platform, unmodified capture, screen rectangle, actor pose, untrimmed frame vs visible bounds, source/native resolution and uncertainty. Compare two independently attested production cases and one original asset at matched screen occupancy. Do not convert nominal author-reported dimensions into observed body height or infer ratio laws from two related titles.

## Outputs and limits

Candidate: an annotated measurement atlas and a set of proportional envelopes with explicit applicability. No global 'ideal sprite size' or fixed arcade-board display contract is authorized by this charter; artist-authored exceptions remain legitimate.

**First bounded inquiry:** select two lawful uncropped reference frames with reliably identified version and camera, measure displayed actor/body bounds separately, and reconcile them with any documented native dimensions.
