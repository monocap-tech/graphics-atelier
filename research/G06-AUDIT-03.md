## Proposed analytic vocabulary (our synthesis)

- **Global support:** occupied spatial envelope, center and direction of extension, silhouette evolution.
- **Internal morphology:** features, tone masses, local deformation and apparent transport; distinguish translation of material from boundary growth.
- **Topology:** emergence, joining, division and disappearance; for flame study track an individual lobe rather than simply compare unrelated contours.
- **Temporal exposure:** distinct drawing/pose, displayed exposure, transition order, spacing, delay and registration against other layers or events.
- **Layer relationships:** independent clocks, correlated peaks, lag, residual matter, foreground/background; phases may overlap and need not form one fixed universal sequence.
- **Context and reception:** apparent material, scale, cause and dramatic meaning; distinguish actual observations, artist intent where evidenced, and viewer reading.

Operational examples: alternate internal contour and shadow-only smoke construction; animate a simplified advected/deforming shape with varying exposure; compare same contour library under different timing; compare matched flash/ring and delayed debris/residue. Establish why a simplification is useful, what breaks at a different size or context, and what requires observation versus authored invention. See [G06-P02](studies/G06-P02.md).
