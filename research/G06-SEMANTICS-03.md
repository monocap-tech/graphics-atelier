## Dated comparisons

C01: Kurita's 1998–99 transfer is directly attested creator process: sample a shared corpus, select recognizable signs, adapt to medium constraints, test with target users. This is a model for design investigation, not a claim that NTT's corpus equals the 2016 Cohn–Ehly sample. Native 12×12 is a historical design constraint, not graphics-wide target. The sign can leave the drawn character and become a textual adjunct. Its audience may change. S1 intent and reported process, not a new S2 visual record.

C02: RC conditional partition (historical simplified categories, exceptions not enumerated):
| Color | Xrd official guide 2016 | Strive launch guide 2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Red | opponent in hit/blockstun or qualifying state | own attack connecting/guarded |
| Yellow | neutral/early action, limited whiff, no qualifying opposing state; 25% tension | own guard; defensive interruption; 50% tension |
| Purple | later whiff / recovery condition | unsuccessful attack etc. |
| Blue | not in enumerated RC vocabulary | neutral / nonaction; slowdown |
The relation changes while the named feature remains; red is broadly conserved, yellow reassigned and old yellow neutral portion now blue. Do not claim full old yellow equivalence to new blue: early action moves to purple or other cases and mechanics differ. Blue Burst is not Blue RC. Strive version-specific changes after 2021 remain out of this comparison. S1 attested historical rule; S02 direct stills 2023 not matched 2016/2021 sequence. SRC161 establishes explicit design inheritance/expansion for Xrd, not specific intent behind later color reassignment.

C03: RPG Maker VX Ace/MV/MZ institutionalizes a graphical vocabulary as an event operation over an entity, with explicit timing control and custom definitions. The MV guide's speaker location example demonstrates functional extension: a character-associated sign can identify participation in a conversation, not just emotion. The older step-forward technique is evidence of a substitutable function with a different graphic realization, not documented equivalence of audience responses. S1 official procedure. No direct historical manga→RPG Maker influence claimed.

C04: Forspoken developers deliberately coordinate magic color, character and system parameter while distinguishing prior franchises. Nature/earth associated with purple rather than assuming green, a counterexample to simplistic universal palette dictionaries. S1 intent and system naming, not visual color measurements or a historical FF mapping comparison.

C05: sparkles in manga/anime may attach to material surfaces, characters, affective background or eyes; in messaging to words and phrases. SRC168 reports new rhetorical emphasis and irony, plus historical associations with beauty/newness and positive affect. S02 VA02 records a specific static crop with an elation label. Primary proposal and exact anime cuts remain open; not newly observed here. Proposed interpretation: polysemy through attachment and use, not necessarily deliberate derivation of every example from a single origin.

## Counterexamples and boundaries

1. Natsume/Miyamoto report earlier frequent usage, different emphases, and omitted comparator. Do not reduce conventionalization to one inventor or count occurrences without conditioning on dramatic opportunities.
2. A shared form and intended meaning need not yield agreement (SRC169–171). Measure agreement within a target audience, dependence on framing, recognition on first encounter vs learned code, and consistency over time separately. A sign may intentionally be ambiguous. Platform redesign and internal game rule changes are distinct sources of semantic drift.
3. RPG Maker makes an existing vocabulary authorable but does not establish that all expressive phenomena should become named event presets. Aesthetic sophistication, original art, dramatic relationships and code utility remain distinct.
4. Do not infer common historical ancestry merely from cross-game similarity. Creator testimony establishes particular intentional transfers; dated recurrence can establish persistence/reassignment; reception and production skill require different evidence.

## Revised reusable analytical record

Four different transformations: (a) **conservation** of a relation through new representation; (b) **extension** of a code by adding members; (c) **reassignment** of a form to a different condition; (d) **pragmatic shift** in the sign's role as attachment or audience changes. These are hypotheses and descriptive classes, not a new DSL. In a production record include code scope/version, intended audience literacy, visual bearer, neighboring alternatives, narrative/interactive relation, invariant, permitted variation, evidence type, and observed failure. A portable sign record is not simply an asset tagged with a noun.
