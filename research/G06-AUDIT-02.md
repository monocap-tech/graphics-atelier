## Synthesis

Production-tool operations, perception of phenomena, authored temporal treatment, and contextual meaning are separate research objects. An identical circle can be a shock wave, a registration cue, a pulsating emblem or an autonomous graphic motif. An event may also have no required informational purpose: ambience, rhythm, visual fascination and experimental abstraction are valid intended ends. Do not assume semantics reduce to gameplay or literal natural phenomena.

The first G06 pass addressed effects transport, compositing and attack semantics. It remains intact as a technical sub-study. The new [effects grammar charter](../docs/EFFECTS_GRAMMAR.md) separates observation/morphology, operations, temporal syntax, expressive systems, contextual transposition, representation, and production. Future study should choose contrasting references before testing, not accumulate arbitrary named effect presets.

Next: G06-A03 investigate effects morphology and timing through natural observation, drawn-animation primary sources, and independent reconstructions. Select original short sequence targets and negative controls. Continue G07 only after a deliberate branch review; no requirement to exhaust the entire effects field first.
