## Use of a case

A case enters an investigation because it bears on an explicit question. Document its version, medium, actual artifact or testimony, method of access, claim and limits. Compare counterexamples when generalizing. Separate (1) source-attested historical practice, (2) inferred mechanism, (3) independently implemented analogy and (4) reproduced transfer. Do not cite exceptional artistic success as proof that a technique is educationally optimal now.

The programme's [governing question](../../docs/PROGRAMME.md) ranges beyond AM2, and its [active investigation order](../ACTIVE.md) is not determined by this index.
