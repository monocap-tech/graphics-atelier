## Cross-track working rule

Before expanding literature, sources or infrastructure, identify whether the open issue is (a) source/provenance, (b) formal/technical, (c) artistic/form, (d) empirical/perception or (e) production/handoff. Choose the corresponding method in [METHODS_AND_QUALITY](../docs/METHODS_AND_QUALITY.md). A priority change updates *this index* and cites its reason; it does not silently rewrite the original study's recorded results or create a global graphics-quality standard.
