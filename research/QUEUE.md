# Bounded work queue

All studies below are proposed, not completed. IDs are provisional; dependencies are soft except where a study explicitly requires a preceding artifact.

| ID | Research question / output | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| G00 | Audit initial sources and search independent artist/homebrew literature across Japanese and English | qualified sources with claims, versions and reproducibility/access flags |
| G01 | Can a small original mechanical sprite preserve silhouette and shading under explicit palette constraints? | Aseprite source, native render, alternative palette and second specimen |
| G02 | Can illustration-derived anime proportions survive raster construction and motion? | original design, scale chart, key poses, animation comparison |
| G03 | Can a rotating model become editable raster frames with acceptable manual effort? | Blender scene, rendering/export recipe, Aseprite cleanup and alternate object |
| G04 | Can modular geometry plus texture detail achieve an intricate arcade environment without modern minimalist appearance? | source scene, budgets and comparison render |
| G05 | Can moving polygon scenery coexist with stable projected gameplay without harming readability? | controlled camera/projection lab; compare composition and performance |
| G06 | Can independently authored effects convey energy, direction and danger without obscuring bullets? | editable effect plus stress scene and non-effect baseline |
| G07 | Can an authored environment yield distinct lighting/dramatic variants reproducibly? | original illustration, perspective base, variants and layer export |
| G08 | Can the target cue and restrained screen design converge? | bridge lab in integration document, including cancellation and alternative treatment |
| G09 | Can selected methods share a display/asset contract? | hybrid demo with clean rerun; only then justify reusable tooling |

## Priority

Start G00 as a bounded targeted pass, then G01/G03 (raster and intermediate conversion), G04/G05 (3D composition) or G08 (presentation bridge) according to available tools. Do not require all literature to be exhausted before making a specimen. Every source sweep ends with a concrete artifact question, a stop decision or a stated blocker.

## Integrated demonstrator candidate

A 30–60 second directed hybrid scene containing illustrative space, an original raster actor, articulated objects, polygonal scenery, procedural effects and a state-bound interface cue. A minimal live interaction may test presentation and cancellation. No campaign or progression requirement. Timing, budgets, style and acceptance thresholds remain provisional until target studies establish them. This demonstrates production integration, not historical equivalence or artistic mastery.
