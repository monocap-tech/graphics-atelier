| [EFFECTS_SEMIOTICS](../docs/EFFECTS_SEMIOTICS.md) | Optional situated-meaning and convention method. SEM-S0…S4 means its former S0…S4; static occurrence does not establish a conventional meaning | Require every effect to signify a discrete state or establish reception |

| [QUEUE](QUEUE.md) / [HANDOFF](../HANDOFF.md) | Current action and blockers at top; previous cursors explicitly historical and non-governing | Follow an obsolete 'next executable' in REV02 or S07 |

**Notation:** EK-A/B/C/D = evidence kind; R0–R5 = maturity of a *specific claim/specimen/workflow*; TC-A/B/C = charter terminal conditions; SEM-S0…S4 = optional meaning/reception evidence; status = proposed/active/executed-bounded/held/blocked/deferred/rejected/closed. Labels are orthogonal, not score components. A tested fixture may be R3 while source fidelity, psychological reception and workflow transfer are all open. Historical A/B/C, S0–S4 and R# references can remain unchanged in their originals.

## Source/provenance distinctions to maintain

- **Creator/professional testimony**: [Nintendo SRC087](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/introduction/design/work04.html), [CEDEC/4Gamer SRC099](https://www.4gamer.net/games/131/G013104/20110906084/), [Sega SRC102](https://cgworld.jp/interview/201911-segagames.html) and [SRC200](https://cgworld.jp/article/202505-cgkiso-2.html); supports intended effects and methods as described, not direct original-sequence measurements or proven reception.
- **Static occurrence**: VA01–14 and VA15 under exact still/crop limitations. VA15 is related to the production but not proven to show the specific encouragement phase. Static recurrence does not imply semantic decoding, denominators or historical circulation.
- **Original runnable tests**: P01, P05–P07, S04a/RT02-R1, S04b; support the precise technical contract they executed. They are not studies of actual viewers and do not become R4 through test counts.
- **External audience research**: [DiGRA JAPAN 2026](https://doi.org/10.57518/digrajprocsummer.2026.0_11) and [Kao 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.entcom.2020.100359). Independent and setting-dependent; not measured project outcomes. Observational review mining such as [2022 impact-feel report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.06155) is not isolated causal attribution.
- **Media gaps**: Nintendo's effects-on/off embedded clips not yet decoded; several registered games represented by static examples/text but no exact matched timed captures; full licensed animation reference material and paid course contents not audited; historical Christie effects term only abstract/lead; runtime/editor access and full clean-clone custody incomplete. Keep each as an explicit blocker instead of inventing a cut or silently substituting a new procedural target.
- **Terminology**: source-language terms are indexed without assuming a single Japanese grammar. Descriptive operations and semantic situations can be reverse lookup axes within the same target record.
