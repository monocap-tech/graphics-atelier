## Interpretive result

Ordinal code: variation within shared motif orders an internal quantity or opportunity. Relational code: pairing and state determine meaning rather than either object's color alone. Indexical/causal event cue: contact star signals a hit; it does not require physically faithful collision material. Identity/register code: same world accommodates stylized and realist treatments, or a distinctive ability shape under varied skins. Affective field: graphic overlay may express an internal condition rather than literal environmental event. These codes can coexist and meaning may be ambiguous or nonpropositional.

These records do not establish historical convergence, frequency, audience recognition, or an effects-specific vocabulary across Japanese games. Important difference: deliberate in-work consistency versus shared convention across works versus actual reception. No deterministic palette dictionary; no forced gameplay utility for autonomous expression.
