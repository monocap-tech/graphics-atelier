## Artistic critique and decision
Contact plate t0/t12/t19/t60 and selected interruptions show glint emphasizing upper right of control and border treating whole control. A reads potentially as a magical flourish or reward cue even though receipt alone is intended; B appears closer to conventional panel focus/response, broad bright perimeter occupies more support so stronger emphasis could simply arise from area. Same scene remains frozen at completion except text/persistent color, so all three articulate request status but none visually establishes the courier–sentinel relation itself. The actual referent has been compressed into an interface proposition. B0 already supplies receipt, target, pending and outcome; no added effect demonstrated necessary. Keep B0 as baseline and conservative default for this fixture only, not a measured audience preference. Both alternate treatments retain experimental value. Need a distinct non-interface study with participants/relations made expressible through the scene instead of through status labels; source acquisition first (see situated cross-index).
