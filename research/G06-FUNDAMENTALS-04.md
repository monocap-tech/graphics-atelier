# G06-F04 — Manga expressive shorthand, gag structure and drawn sound in context

Date: 2026-11-18. **Status: source-led and image-qualified F04 foundations.** R1 for publisher, institutional, creator and instructor testimony; small **static visual** records as separately indicated. No original art/software prototype, authoring proficiency, timed animation, audience response, complete purchased book or proven manga→anime→game lineage. Continues [F01 page](G06-FUNDAMENTALS-01.md) → [F02 composition](G06-FUNDAMENTALS-02.md) → [F03 material](G06-FUNDAMENTALS-03.md). [STD01](../docs/EFFECTS_STANDARD.md) remains the eventual production evaluation standard, not an instruction to prototype at every discovery.

## Governing question

How do short-lived or visually economical manga constructions—漫符 (*manpu*), 形喩 (*keiyu*), effect lines, expressive sound lettering and simple marks—operate **in relation to characters, panels, reading order, speech, background, black/tone treatment and gag rhythm**? Which meanings depend on visual distribution, learned association and the sequence's setup? We do not presuppose that a mark is an independent asset or that the convention maps to a single emotion or literal sound. This is the user's requested foundation, not a new universal effects grammar.

## Bounded close observations and careful inferences

### F04-V01: count-dependent meaning (quoted, not page-observed)

- **Original text**: Kōno's ♪ in the publisher excerpt: one frequently points to buoyancy or ease; multiple frequently suggest music sounding. [F04-S01].
- **Variation of form**: one element vs a group, and implied acoustic/affective status.
- **Inference**: the sign is not synonymous with an item-class 'music', even within this creator's account; distribution and context matter. **Do not** generalize that all single notes are moods, all plural notes music, or that human readers always perceive the distinction.
- **Missing**: paired full-page examples and reader judgments.

### F04-V02: 'Zzz' at a four-panel ending (small full-strip secondary photograph)

- [F04-S12] left-hand four-panel column visibly depicts two boardgame participants progressively still or tiring; floating/repeated Latin Z shapes are prominent toward the quiet/drowsy final encounter. Four-panel sequence and visual change already supply context.
- The marks are not proof of recorded in-world acoustics; their temporal/psychological role is grasped in relation to poses and earlier activity. The image's orientation and low resolution prevent claims of precise chronological panel lettering and full story transcription.
- A photograph of a printed catalogue spread **does not equal a direct reading of the original printed manga or its manuscript**.

### F04-V03: written silence and nonliteral impact (institutional source, panels not independently observed)

- METI's *Manga grammar* discusses 'シーン' to make **silence** explicit even though the page is physically noiseless. A reader can have an expectation of unmarked background sound; adding a written non-sound changes the represented experience. Interpretive claim by exhibition, not measured reception.
- It also describes Araki's use of gunshot-like lettered sound on a kiss as an expressive exaggeration, not documentary acoustics; original example p.81 cited by exhibit but not opened independently.
- Implication: an effect can **construct the sensory register of the scene**, not simply transcribe a physical phenomenon. This bears on the user's psychological-efficacy observation, but does not establish how a particular spectator feels.

### F04-V04: gag structure vs gadget (single page screenshot)

- F04-S08's *Fuku-chan* lesson explicitly presents **起** (what begins), **承** (development), **転** (unexpected shift), **結** (reveal/punchline) and invites deviation from that template. In its example, the final image explains previous behaviors as an experiment about being triggered by water.
- The important artistic resource is *temporally controlled knowledge*: what is revealed, withheld or reinterpreted between panels; the physical number of marks is not the humor mechanism.
- Do not equate four panels with four animation phases or claim every joke obeys a single shape.

## Provisional synthesis grounded in observations

1. **Simple graphical marks rely on composition and learned context for their function.** The same ♪ can qualify mood or audible music; 'シーン' turns absence into an explicit expressive presence.
2. **The host is not merely a storage location.** Facial expression, pose, relation, balloons, prior and subsequent panels, solid black, silence and timing shape how a mark is read. A copied symbol asset may fail if its panel/sequence context is absent.
3. **Graphic writing is not reducible to text nor sound.** Shape, placement, density, black/tone material and lettering provide expressive modulation; conventions can be played against literal sound. Relevant to future audiovisual transfer, not proof that animation/games historically inherited it.
4. **Gags depend on withholding, timing, reinterpretation and tone.** Four-panel structure is one teachable model, and deviations are attested. A powerful effect can be the placement/removal of a mark, a quiet panel or a final context shift rather than elaborate VFX.
5. **Practice varies by artist.** Kōno's own 2025 interview says she rarely uses screentone, even while F03's history shows tone production proliferated. **Do not confuse access to a tool with necessary use of it or artistic superiority**.
6. **Gag makers can explicitly analyze or violate conventions from inside the form.** Itō's account of Akatsuka and later metacomic work demonstrates a distinct craft question: comic effect may arise from **breaking the reader's prediction of manga's rules**, rather than adding a recognized sign. This is art-historical criticism, not a systematic chronology or reception measure.
7. **Genealogy is contested and multidirectional.** The exhibition explicitly names older European/US comics and animation influences, while 2026 scholarship disputes abrupt claims about introduction dates. Sound recording, lettering, print, live presentation, animation, film and books may all matter. No single direct manga→anime→game descent is presently shown.
