# G06-P06 — Shearwake: contour versus analytic flow

Executed locally with Python, Pillow 12.3.0 and NumPy 2.3.5. Native 160x90 transparent RGBA, binary alpha with four opaque palette colors, 48 ticks at 24 Hz, 4x logical upscale. Comparison GIF uses 2x panels and 48 frames / 2000 ms (verified duration with 40/50 ms GIF holds). Two native sheets 1280x540, 8 columns x 6 rows, untrimmed cell index t at (160*(t%8),90*floor(t/8)); top-left origin. Duplicate poses deliberately retained. Timing source t/24 s.
