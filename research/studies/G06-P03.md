# G06-P03 — Synchrony, identity and audiovisual score

2026-11-18. Six mathematical checks executed; original score SVG and browser preview authored. No browser/audio playback, editor project or participant evaluation executed. No third-party assets. This extends G06-P02 exposure controls to intermodal mapping.

## Fixture

Four seconds / 96 authoring ticks at 24 Hz; eight abstract sound events at ticks 3+12i, two voice labels A/B, balanced counts, order AABABBAB. Visual motifs square/ring, six-tick pulse each, matched event IDs. Declared relation A→square/B→ring. Four visual treatments: declared mapping, consistent global reassignment, inconsistent reassignment, and declared mapping delayed fixed 3 ticks (125 ms). All motifs and counts held constant. Exact onset count is paired by authored event ID, not a real alignment algorithm. Best bijective matches is the maximum over the two possible one-to-one label mappings, only meaningful for this restricted categorical fixture.

| Treatment | Exact onsets (of 8) | Declared mapping matches | Best fixed bijection | Mean absolute lag (ticks) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| declared_mapping | 8 | 8 | 8 | 0 |
| global_reassignment | 8 | 0 | 8 | 0 |
| inconsistent_reassignment | 8 | 4 | 4 | 0 |
| delayed_mapping | 0 | 8 | 8 | 3 |

Six checks passed in JS sandbox; no Node CLI separately run. This establishes independence of defined timing and mapping measures, not the quality, meaningfulness or perceived synchrony of a composition. Inconsistent means inconsistent with any fixed bijection in this toy model, not artistic error. Stable global reassignment is equally systematic; the lagged treatment may be intentional. Repeated presentation, order effects, unequal acoustic/visual salience and unblinded labels confound actual viewer evaluation.

## Proposed operator/transfer study

Create a short original 8–16 second audio/visual phrase using two original sound sources and related graphic motifs. Plan events by beat/measure, label source identity and visual operation, rough storyboard, V-con, then finalize art. Compare variants with the same assets, total duration and event bias; change only mapping, lag, or phrase ordering at a time. Include silent baseline and an equally coherent alternative mapping; do not suggest there is a universal crossmodal correspondence. Test two distinct realization methods (drawn frame sequence with exposure sheet vs AE/parametric vectors) and port a related operation to a game-independent graphic phrase or state-driven targeting cue. If testing people, use an appropriate blind/counterbalanced procedure with interpretation questions, not a binary synchrony metric. Preserve timings, original sounds/graphics, source and exports, rights, critiques and competing explanations. Fixed audio, authored score and simulation event ownership should remain separately declared.

Acceptance for this prototype: event schedule and identity checks, later browser validation and actual motion/audio capture; design-level acceptance needs reasoned interpretation and transfer. No editor/runtime certification or perceptual claims.
