# G06-S04 — Proposed radiance contrast study

2026-11-18. Preregistration draft, not executed. Local assistant-selected design direction for review, no project-wide style or dimensions approved.

## Question
Does a recognizable luminous motif retain its appearance while changes in bearer/context alter its intended significance, and does an assigned sign survive a change of graphic realization? Test necessity of animation rather than assume it is ornamental or sufficient.

## Bounded reference
VA12 (material glow), VA02 (affective background), VA13/14 (identity distinction). SRC174 healing and SRC180 counter window are documented functions, not yet observed reference sequences. Acquire exact lawful source cuts and alternative examples before claiming motion reconstruction; if unavailable limit experiment to independent static/temporal semantic contrast, not faithful commercial reproduction. No traced assets.

## Original specimen and matched comparisons
Author an original illustrated character with a small reflective ornament, an empty surrounding field, and a separate opponent/device in a compact dramatic scene. Create shared four-point highlight library with independently editable source and named anchors. First render a static composition with ornament-attached motif, then field-attached motif in same scene (same motif count, pixel area, color, scale and placement-distance where feasible, report remaining confounds). Include no-motif base; compare context-only and motif-only panels. Extend to an original short anticipation/action scene with actor relation, a glint that coincides with a designated counter window, and an intentionally mistimed version. Neutral static highlight in same scene acts as distractor. Compare matched-duration held and animated glints, not different effects families. Optional alternative geometry (ring or non-star glint) within the same event mapping. A separate aesthetic direction can retain material/character identity without any discrete state indication.

## Acceptance before rendering
- Exact editable source, palette, layer stack, anchor and cue ownership; compare static exports in same viewport and background; no geometry reuse claim if source differs.
- Record graphic extent, number of marks, composition, frame exposure and real sampling; compare all-tick event/visual channel and composed pixels for interactive treatment, including false cue from repeat/cancel/expired window, moving actor, effects toggle and background changes. Independent oracle from declared event schedule, not the renderer's returned state. Mutation checks include misplaced cue, one-tick early/late, stuck and missing cue. RT01 P07 remediation remains separate.
- Expert/author critique should examine material plausibility, adjacency, placement, whether motif supports or overwhelms art, dramatic interpretation and alternative readings; no preference/reception inference from timestamps or mask counts.
- For any later voluntary perception study: unlabelled stills and silent sequences, randomized/counterbalanced conditions, open interpretation then separate state/valence questions, unfamiliar viewers vs instructed code; preregister primary endpoint and uncertainty/sample plan before recruiting, report individual disagreement and limits. No participant data currently.
- Transfer target: alternate actor design and a different visual realization with invariant counter availability relation; independently judge whether aesthetic intention survives. Preserve source, exported artifact, revision costs and runtime settings. Production sufficiency requires real DCC/host import and review beyond browser mocks.

Stop after this bounded study and review rather than build a radiance generator or universal taxonomy. Predeclare target quality criteria against selected references before actual execution; no user-approved threshold currently.
