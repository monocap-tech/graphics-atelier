# G06-P04 — Compositional voice, recurrence and variation

2026-11-18. Original numerical control passed in JS sandbox; SVG plate and local browser preview authored, playback not tested. No external assets. Inspired by operation in SRC128, not a reconstruction of its choreography.

## Fixture and results

One 24-tick source phrase at 24 Hz, piecewise linear trajectory through five authored knots, moving 4 units per tick in x and with changing y direction. Three voices active for a half-open local interval 0<=u<24. Shared 42-tick observation window, circles radius 8 at sampled positions (not actual game collision shapes). Compare 6-tick delayed copies, 2-tick crowded copies, 6-tick delay plus middle-voice vertical reflection, and 6-tick delay plus middle-voice 2x playback. For each sampled tick, count circle overlaps (center distance strictly <16); report minimum center distance over concurrent pairs and number of simultaneous voice ticks. All paths are original and centered in a 160x144 local viewing canvas. Numerical test measures sample positions only; no continuous collision detection or visual perception claimed.

| Variant | Min distance | Overlap ticks | Concurrent (>=2) ticks | All three ticks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| canon | 24 | 0 | 24 | 12 |
| crowded | 8 | 24 | 24 | 20 |
| mirrored | 24 | 0 | 24 | 12 |
| diminished | 0 | 5 | 18 | 6 |

The 6-tick copy case preserves x spacing 24 by construction; crowding produces overlaps. Reflection preserves x spacing. Acceleration catches up with an earlier voice despite unchanged onset delay; this is neither automatically undesirable nor desirable. Six sanity checks passed, including shared path transformations and early ending of the accelerated voice. Delays and transformations change composition while reusing the same phrase. Counts do not independently establish counterpoint, musicality or readability; temporal variation can alter event density and lifespan, so these are not matched perceptual controls.
