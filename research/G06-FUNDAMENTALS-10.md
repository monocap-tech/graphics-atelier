## Historical target and qualification

Desired observation: one successful shot of an airborne enemy during an early ordinary stage, with the **preceding targeting situation, projectile, hit, falling/exploding graphic, ground event, and the next visible enemy** continuously in view; include shadow/ground/camera and sound where available. ## Engineering evidence and non-evidence

MAME *emulates* a sprite pipeline able to position, scale, flip, choose bank/palette and mix shadows. Two separate source files document that code; it does **not** prove how Sega encoded the explosion's **behavior**. The IGCC author's statement that an effect can substitute for its defeated foe without spawning more objects is a **reasonable rendering-load hypothesis**. To prove it, obtain a gameplay set and observable sprite list/trace across hit events; inspect the count, bank/address, position/scale, priority and palette of rendered sprites, and distinguish newly allocated objects from updates of the same slot. Even successful evidence would establish observed runtime operation, **not automatically the original artist's reason**.

**Modern direct-emulator visible region:** MAME configured `set_raw(25.1748MHz/4, 400, 0, 320, 262, 0, 224)`, while older emulator metadata mentions a 304×224 visible area. Do not assert historical pixel dimensions or scale from a single emulator screenshot. Record actual source viewport, border/crop and recording transformations.

**Formal limits:** MAME `screen_update` composites road, tilemap/text and sprite with priority, and shade effects. This supports a **layer architecture**, not exact sprite-to-world 3D projection equations, actor depth, collision math, or an editor-based original production recipe. Differences among arcade PCB variants, ROMs, emulator versions and ports remain a significant limitation. Do not conflate retroengine emulator code with Sega's original game code.

## Next bounded observation protocol — exact clip (not executed)

**Acceptance condition** before moving F10 to a fully observed temporal record: find a **lawful continuous 5–10 s arcade-era-equivalent encounter** with at least one unambiguous airborne enemy kill and ground-blast aftermath; known video and build source, visible stage, sufficiently unobscured scene, native or losslessly rescaled capture if possible, audio. More than one encounter may be needed for A/B versus D/E. If no source qualifies, mark as BLOCKED, not interpolated.

A hypothetical extraction log should track:
- Unique source/URL, archive/checksum when accessible, video version/date, arcade set (`sharrier` vs `sharrier1` or unknown), provenance of emulator/port and postprocessing (aspect/crop/scaler/interpolation), playback frame rate and whether frames are duplicated.
- Exact video timestamp and sequential decoded **frame numbers**. Keep on-screen event clock distinct from video file clock and output refresh.
- Target enemy identity, world/screen position and relative apparent size, trajectory and shadow before hit; screen-occupying other foes/projectiles; grid/sky/camera origin and ground reference.
- Targeting cue/audio timestamp vs visible alignment, bullet onset/path and contact; distinguish bullet visual from inferred collision instant and cue latency.
- Enemy graphic → explosion graphic transition (whether overlap, single frame substitution, or stagger), its screen/world motion down, scale/palette/contrast, when ground-specific drawing appears, how long remains and when clears.
- **Occlusion:** identify one rear enemy in same local area and whether visible each event phase. Do not declare improved readability from a single image unless sufficient comparator is available.
- Independent comparison: an indestructible target with ricochet+sound, or a boss/large explosion. Do not compare mismatched ports as matched controls.
- No frame numbers/durations may be entered from unviewed YouTube metadata or reviewer prose. Actual per-frame notes, human interpretation, correctness tests and conjectured code shall occupy distinct sections.

**Decision after acquisition:** is it a sprite-sequence/movement construction, a world-event transition, an audio-driven cue, a priority/occlusion art-direction problem—or several coordinated elements? Then one **editable original** candidate can be scoped under [STD01](../docs/EFFECTS_STANDARD.md). The F10 report itself **does not** authorize a reconstruction prototype or imply sufficient graphics production skill. If longplay frames cannot be decoded here, preserve current R1 account and obtain suitable access; do not pretend a timestamped empirical observation occurred.
