## Synthesis

Effects are a combination of authored vocabulary (texture, geometry, emission, motion, sound), event semantics (warning, activation, impact, cancellation, aftermath) and compositing constraints. For a reverse STG, separate authoritative hazard geometry/onset from its readable core and nonessential decorative extensions. Semantic correspondence does not imply every glow texel is damaging. Preserve warnings and active boundaries; decorative treatment should not conceal them or incorrectly imply active hazards. World effect, scene-dependent effect, HUD feedback and simulation object are separate ownership decisions.

Author assets against the actual environment from G04/G05 rather than a black editor preview alone. Compare simultaneous projectile density and bright backgrounds; large effects behind bullet cores are necessary in some proposed setups but not sufficient for local contrast. Keep linear/sRGB, HDR/clamp, blending, depth, logical resolution and sampling explicit; export recipe is part of the asset contract. Mixed normal/additive replay and distortion need distinct treatment. See [G06-P01](studies/G06-P01.md); the scalar test is a counterexample under stated conditions, not a measure of viewer recognition or an Effekseer renderer test.
