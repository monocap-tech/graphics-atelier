# G06-S03b — Radiance as an expressive family

2026-11-18. Bounded qualitative source-led study; not a corpus frequency estimate, physical optics reconstruction or new audience test. Extend S02/S03. Radiance (輝き・きらめき) is a provisional comparison family broader than isolated star-shaped sparkles, not a claim that all luminous phenomena share one sign or genealogy. Distinguish glitter, specular highlight, glint, glow, expressive field and literal light emission.

## Comparative interpretation
| Support/relationship | Possible function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Surface and illumination | material gloss/brilliance | VA12, SRC172–173 |
| Character-peripheral field | affective emphasis | VA02, SRC136 |
| Costume/environment/photographic composite | thematic affiliation | SRC175 |

| Exceptional entity's body | supernatural status | SRC174 summons |
| Entrance plus identity | rarity/variant indication | SRC177–178 |
| Location or moving cluster | discovery affordance | SRC179 |
| Enemy anticipation and brief window | counter opportunity and impending danger | SRC180 |
| Board state and actor relation | attack success or threat to recipient | SRC181 |

Provisional common relation: luminous differentiation may elevate an entity, event or region relative to its context, but it need not be positive, imply literal emission or have discrete semantics. SHIKA-Q explicitly exemplifies opposite valence by actor perspective. GOBBLE's glint predicts an opportunity during a hostile action; contrast FF healing. Neither proves identical morphology. Gem example supports optionality; shape alone is insufficient, timing may be constitutive. Static image examples establish support and appearance, not historical transfer from shōjo manga. Sacred/ominous art with directly observed matched references remains unfulfilled. Do not treat special status, material shine, affect and attention as equivalent or call all light a sparkle.
