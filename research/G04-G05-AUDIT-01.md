## Synthesis (our inference)

Camera direction, active gameplay plane, narrative timing and background construction are coupled. First establish what must remain operable and readable throughout a directed passage; then block out the sequence, refine camera and enemy/FX timing together, and construct only visible environment details needed across the camera envelope. Do not conflate authentic texture-driven detail with generic low-poly minimalism or hardware distortion. Shared palettes can establish material roles (cf. G01), trim sheets add repeated structural detail, unique art supplies landmarks; choose per surface. Preserve source meshes/UVs, textures and stage timeline independently so scaling/porting does not demand a full redraw.
