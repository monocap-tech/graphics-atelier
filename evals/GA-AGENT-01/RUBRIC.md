## Pass criteria (each required)

1. **Source retrieval (0/1):** cites at least two relevant exact research files/sections, including one actual executed specimen, and correctly says what each establishes. A vague “according to the repo” fails.
2. **Evidence calibration (0/1):** separates measured raster/pivot/geometry facts from unknown artistic recognition, any unrun Unity import and any unproven historical method. No phantom tools.
3. **Native-vs-adapted reasoning (0/1):** distinguishes purpose-led native-canvas design from rasterizing/vector-adapting a finished illustration, without forbidding either.
4. **Consequential alternatives (0/1):** offers at least two alternatives differing in actual structure (e.g. silhouette proportions, feature allocation, pose/camera relation), not merely palette or antialiasing.
5. **Conditional decision (0/1):** grounds any choice in intended display context, identifies at least one cost and a falsifying observation; explicitly avoids proclaiming 48px, 192px cell or 112px displayed height universal.
6. **Bounded execution honesty (0/1):** does not claim to have created artwork or connected an editor; proposes a feasible next comparison and recording method.

**Pass:** all six must score 1. **Revise once:** if any fail, identify whether error is retrieval, evidence taxonomy, alternative generation or model reasoning; make at most one targeted change to instructions/index/skill, then repeat with a fresh agent. **Stop:** if the second run fails, log the specific unresolved defect and decline to build an orchestration framework or claim an artistic-capability result from this test.
